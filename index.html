Questions people ask

Is a reading a prediction?

No. Tarot and astrology show the energy around a situation and help you see your options clearly. Your choices still shape what happens.

What can I ask about?

Love, relationships, career direction, self-growth, family, and anything you need clarity on. I don't read on health, legal cases or money and investments. Please speak to a professional for those.

Do I need my birth time?

It's optional. Your date of birth is enough for a tarot reading. Birth time and place let me add your rising sign and more astrology detail.

How will I receive it?

By email or Instagram DM, whichever you choose. You get the cards drawn and a written message for each question.
